DESCRIPTION: in 2022. The building is 3 stories with the lower level below grade and the first

BUDGET: $17,000,000.00
PROJECT STATUS: On time and on budget
SUB PROJECT: 2025 Building Renovation - Dream Accelerator
DESCRIPTION: This project will include additional renovations to the lower-level totaling
approximately 6,000 SF for the “Dream Accelerator” area only. The Junior
Achievement space in the northeast area will have displays and an interactive
museum like experience, similar to a facility researched in Denver. The
furnishings, technology, and interactive display features will be designed and
provided by others (1220 Exhibits Inc). An estimated occupant load of 104 has
been discussed for this space. Mechanical, Electrical, Fire Protection, and
Technology updates to serve the Dream Accelerator space are also included.
