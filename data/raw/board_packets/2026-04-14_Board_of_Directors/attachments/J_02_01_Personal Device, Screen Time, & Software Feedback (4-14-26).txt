Software Feedback
● Part of the budget reductions identified reducing the cost of
software used in our district
● All software programs and systems were reviewed to identify
potential reductions based on:
○ Redundancy
○ Impact on student learning
○ Impact on system efficiency
● We are seeking feedback from staff on potential alternatives
when considering replacement of specific programs

Screen Time Feedback
● Feedback from the community identified a desire to review the
use of screens in schools for instructional purposes
● Additional budget reductions can be achieved by revising our
district’s 1:1 initiative
● We are seeking feedback from our staff and families regarding
the use of screens at school
● We will utilize a committee, and the above feedback, to help
guide future decisions
