KPI source-trace report

Every scorecard number, traced from primary source through formula to output.

What this page is. A line-by-line audit trail for the financial-KPI scorecard. Every figure below is recomputed here from its raw audited/filed components, so the published number reproduces in front of you, and each one links back to the primary source document. A reporter can verify the scorecard without taking our word for anything.

How to verify any number in four steps

  1. Read the value on the scorecard (or in kpi_scorecard.csv).
  2. Apply the formula below — the same one in the builder kpi_scorecard.py — with the raw components shown.
  3. Open the extraction the components come from (Iowa_City_CSD.csv / unspent-authorized-budget.csv).
  4. Trace to the primary document — the ICCSD ACFR (RSM US LLP) or the Iowa DOM filing — listed in the provenance index at the bottom.

About. ICCSD Forecasting is an unofficial analysis project that extracts Iowa City Community School District financial data from public records and builds a transparent General Fund cash forecast. It is not affiliated with ICCSD, Moody’s, S&P, or PFM. Figures are estimates carrying material uncertainty; nothing here is investment advice.
